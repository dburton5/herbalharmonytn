What we’re looking for

Informational, educational writing about herbs and everyday wellness, from people who live in Memphis. A plant you brew, a family recipe, a Sunday ritual, a plain-language walk through something you researched.

  • Your own experience and words. First person is welcome.
  • Practical detail a neighbor could use: how you brew it, when you reach for it, what you skip.
  • Sources when you mention a study, a book, or a tradition. A link or a citation is enough.
  • A short bio and the neighborhood you want next to your name.

What we won’t publish

  • Medical claims. Don’t say an herb diagnoses, treats, cures, or prevents a disease.
  • Dosing instructions presented as treatment, or “this replaced my medication.”
  • Copied articles, AI paste with no personal point of view, or product pitches dressed up as stories.
  • Pieces from outside Memphis. This desk is for the city.

How a pitch becomes a post

  1. You send the form. It lands with Don, not on the site.
  2. He reads it for fit, clarity, and safety. He may edit or ask you to revise.
  3. If he says yes, the story is published under Community Voices with your name, photo, and neighborhood, plus a “Reviewed by” line.
  4. If it’s not a fit, you’ll hear that too. Silence is not a yes.

Your email is only used to reply about this submission. It is not added to the product newsletter.
