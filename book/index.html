Most fasting advice tells you how long to go without food. This book shows you what your body is actually doing along the way, phase by phase, from your last meal to your first bite back, and how to move through each one with less struggle.

What's Inside

Six phases. One map of your fast.

Every fast moves through the same stages. Knowing where you are, and what's coming next, is what turns a hard fast into a steady one.

0–3 hours

Fed State

Your body digests and stores your last meal. What you eat here sets up how smoothly the whole fast goes.

3–12 hours

Post-Absorptive

Digestion wraps up and your liver starts drawing on stored glycogen. Mild hunger comes in waves and passes.

12–24 hours

Early Fasting

Glycogen runs low and your body leans into fat for fuel. Hunger often peaks here, and this is where most people quit.

About 18–48 hours

Ketosis

Your liver turns fat into ketones. Many people describe steadier energy and an appetite that finally quiets down.

48–72+ hours

Autophagy

Your cells break down and recycle worn-out parts, a deep housekeeping process. Fasts this long call for a doctor's OK first.

Over 4–24 hours

Refeeding

How you break a fast decides how good you feel after it. The book walks you through it hour by hour.
